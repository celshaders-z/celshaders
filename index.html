[This review will have spoilers] A week ago, I got really into a fangame for an ARG, the ARG being INTERLOPER by anomidae, and the fangame being SOURCEVOID ONLINE by 333 SOFTWARE. To save you a 7 hour lore recap, the “source void” is a large interconnected void full of maps from the source engine. Places from games like: Half-life 2, it’s episodes, Portal (I don’t know about portal 2), Team Fortress 2, Counter Strike Source, Garry’s mod (this includes community made maps), as well as others. The game is always online, (hence the name) and you put in “seeds” to spawn in a random location in the void and meet up with your friends in the same location. You can pick up props and use your flashlight, move around, and fall from maps to escape entities that will chase you. These entities include:
Umbrella Men - From the ARG, they act as predators in the void, their presence are made known by a jingle.
VRAD errors - error models that act similarly to Umbrella Men, they also play a sound when approaching.
Zombies/Mimics - When you are killed by an Umbrella Men or VRAD errors, you leave behind a mangled corpse. If you wait around by one of these for long enough they will wake up and begin to chase you.
ALL THE ABOVE ENTITIES WILL CAUSE YOU TO CRASH.
Player Mimics - I have yet to encounter one, other than hearing them several times, but they repeat sentences players say.
Cones - I have seen a cone twice, once where it came back several times. From what I can tell, It acts very similar to a developer, so I am not yet convinced it is an npc. It can spawn in explosive barrels, hover outside a map, Repeat a lot of sentences, like DEAD MAN BAD or KILL MAN BAD.
Trolls - Not a real entity. It was a patreon member, (patreon members can change their models and play sound effects), and one of them went invisible (bug) and played several sound effects.
Overall, the game really makes you feel like you’re in an ARG, and it’s fun to chat with people while you’re falling, and people in the community are very nice. Although the prop-grabbing physics are weird, due to server time,
